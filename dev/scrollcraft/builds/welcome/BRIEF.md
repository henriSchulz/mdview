# Brief: the welcome page (md.henrischulz.com, not signed in)

Interviewed on 2026-10-06, four questions answered by Henri; the rest authored within them and
marked so. The page itself is in `web/` (`lib/welcome.ts`, `public/welcome/`, `public/host/demo.js`).

## Answered

- **Material**: "Die echte App": the page shows mdview itself, rendered live. No generated imagery, no key.
- **Structure**: "Die Seite ist eine Notiz": scrolling writes a note before the visitor's eyes, and at the end it is shared.
- **Action**: "Beides nebeneinander": `Sign in with GitHub` and `Get the desktop app`, as equals, at the end.
- **Language and place**: English, at `/` for whoever is not signed in.

## Authored (not asked)

- **Vibe**: quiet, exact, a tool on a desk. The app's own colours and motion (`motion.css`); nothing the app would not have.
- **Energy**: busiest at the start (the typing), calm in the middle, still at the end.
- **Range**: premium-minimal, because the surface is the app and the app is.
- **Assets**: none besides the app. The copy is the sample notes.
- **Believe by the end**: these are my files, and this is a real editor for them.

## Grammar

Live surface. The other seven lost because the honest pitch is "watch what it does": there is no
footage to scrub (filmic, world), no catalogue (gallery), and a headline over the app would be
marketing chrome the app does not have (poster, editorial, split, cutlist).

## Feeling curve

1. Write (pin, 3.6): curiosity, then delight. The wheel types a note into the real app; scrolling back unwrites it.
2. Files (flow): calm. A document to read, the one place the page states facts in prose.
3. Share (pin + wipe, 2.8): confidence. The app's own share window gives a link that works; the wipe shows what `/raw` returns, fetched.
4. Places (pan, 4.2): breadth. Five places, one set of notes.
5. Try (pin, 1.3): resolve. The editor, with a cursor in it, and the two ways on.

**Peak**: act 1. "I scrolled and the page typed a note into the actual app, formula and all, and when I scrolled back it deleted it."
It is the first act, so nothing quieter stands before it; the page opens on the note already begun instead.

**Tell-someone sentence**: It's the site where your scroll wheel is the keyboard.

**Signature move**: the scroll position is the note's length. One character per few pixels, both ways, into the app's own renderer.

**Authored silence**: none.

## Changed after Henri saw it (2026-10-06)

- "Zu doll die App": the page opens as a landing page now. A claim, the two ways in, a cue, pieces of Markdown in the air, and the app leaning back under the fold. This leaves the live-surface grammar's bans on purpose.
- The copy speaks of the app, not of the scrolling.
- The share act and the rail of five places are gone. In their place: a tour of what a note can hold (pin, 4.4; sharing is its last, quiet stop), and a section on open source (flow).
